The solution

One product,
three honest promises.

FlexQ shifts mental health care from episodic treatment to continuous presence. Not a mood tracker. Not a diagnostic tool. A clinical signal layer.

For patients

For you in every moment.

60-second daily check-in. Your therapist sees what you choose. Your employer never does. No streaks, no shaming.

For therapists

Walk into every session already oriented.

Triage by change, not score. 90-second pre-session brief. Adapt plans with AI assistance you control and sign off on.

For enterprises

A leading indicator, not a wellness app.

Aggregated, anonymised workforce signal — useful to HR, defensible to regulators, invisible at the individual level.

How it works?

Grounded in recognised
therapeutic approaches.

Developed in collaboration with CBT and clinical experts. Every flag shows its rule — no black boxes.

01 · Capture

Quick daily check-in

60 seconds — mood, energy, stress, sleep, optional reflection. Skipping is safe.

02 · Compare

Baseline-relative

Patient compared to themselves — not a population norm. Personal baseline, persistence detection.

03 · Surface

Make patterns visible

Persistent deviations flagged with the rule that ggered them. Transparent. Inspectable.

04 · Act

Therapist-led action

Acknowledge, adapt, or dismiss. No autonomous AI care. Full audit trail.
