Who builds FlexQ

Built with clinicians,
not just for them.

FlexQ is developed in close collaboration with licensed psychologists, CBT practitioners, and occupational health researchers. Every feature goes through clinical review before it ships.

"A tool that sits between sessions has to earn the trust of both sides of the room — the therapist and the client. That takes clinical rigour, not just good UX."

Mental health technology has a credibility problem. Too many products make clinical-sounding claims without any clinical grounding. We built FlexQ differently: the methodology comes first, the product follows. Our advisory board reviews the signal logic, the flag thresholds, the ACE prompt architecture, and the language we use with patients — before any of it reaches users.

Clinical & Psychology

Practitioners who
use what they build.

Our clinical team consists of licensed psychologists and CBT specialists with active practices. They validate every instrument, flag threshold, and piece of patient-facing language before it reaches users — and they continue reviewing how features perform after launch.

This is not an advisory panel brought in to sign off on decisions already made. They are embedded in the product process from the first brief.

Areas of expertise on our advisory board

Clinical psychology

Licensed clinical psychologists who work with patients daily. They validate that FlexQ's signal logic reflects how deterioration actually presents — not how it looks in a textbook.

CBT & practice

Cognitive-behavioural therapy specialists who ensure our check-in structure and ACE plan drafts are grounded in evidence-based frameworks — not generic wellness prompts.

Occupational health

Workplace mental health researchers who shape our enterprise product. They define what aggregate signals are clinically meaningful for HR — and which ones would be misleading or harmful.

Research & psychometrics

Academic researchers in psychology and psychometrics who review our measurement approach. The baseline model and flag thresholds are tested against published clinical literature before deployment.

Ethics & data governance

Legal and ethics advisors who review every data flow for GDPR compliance, patient autonomy, and clinical duty of care — particularly where AI-assisted features intersect with therapeutic relationships.

We work with a small group of clinical advisors and are selectively open to new collaborations — particularly from practitioners with active caseloads.

How we work together

Clinical review is built
into the process, not bolted on.

01
Feature specificationEvery new feature starts with a clinical brief — what problem does this solve, what signals does it rely on, and what could go wrong if it misfires.
02
Advisory reviewRelevant advisors review the specification. Flag thresholds, ACE prompts, and patient-facing language are all approved before development begins.
03
Practitioner testingWorking therapists test the feature against real caseload scenarios before release. We look for false positives, confusing UI, and anything that would undermine clinical trust.
04
Ongoing monitoringAfter launch, advisors review how the feature is used in practice. If signal logic drifts or generates unexpected patterns, thresholds are recalibrated.
