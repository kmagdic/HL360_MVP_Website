Who we are

FlexQ Health ("FlexQ", "we", "us") is the data controller for this marketing website. We operate a hybrid mental health platform — this page covers the marketing site only, not the platform itself.

02

What we collect

We collect data in two ways only.

Analytics

When you accept analytics cookies, our analytics tools collect anonymised data about your visit: pages viewed, time on site, approximate country, device type, and referring source.

IP addresses are anonymised before storage. No data is linked to your identity. We use this to understand which parts of the site are useful.

No analytics cookies are set before you consent.

Email address

If you submit a demo request or join the waitlist, we collect your email address and the message you choose to send. Nothing else.

We use your email to respond to your enquiry and, with your agreement, to send occasional product updates. You can unsubscribe from updates at any time via the link in any email we send.

We do not share your email with any third party. We do not sell data.
