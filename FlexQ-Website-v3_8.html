The problem

Therapy is episodic.
Mental strain isn't.

2–4
Weeks between sessions. The visibility window is empty — a client can deteriorate in silence for most of it.
30–40%
Of session time spent reconstructing what happened in between. Therapy time spent on administration.
Most
Deterioration goes silent until a crisis forces it into view — when intervention is late and harder.

"The first 10–15 minutes of every session used to be reconstruction. Now it's therapy. That alone is worth it."

A client may start sleeping worse, losing energy, or feeling overwhelmed days before the next appointment. FlexQ closes that gap — making the invisible visible before the next session.

Value drivers

High-value talent retention

The most critical lever

Detect sustained strain elevation or early burnout signals early enough for leadership to intervene. Preventing even a handful of high-value exits annually can offset the entire platform investment.

02

Sick-leave reduction

A real, but secondary lever

Even reducing one sick day per employee annually generates meaningful savings at enterprise scale. Sick leave is a visible cost — presenteeism is the larger hidden one.

03

Presenteeism stabilisation

The largest hidden cost

Employees under sustained mental strain remain at work but operate below full cognitive capacity. Small productivity improvements across a large population yield significant returns.
