04 · Risk management

Know who needs attention before it becomes a crisis.

FlexQ watches your entire caseload continuously. When a client crosses a risk threshold — sustained deterioration, missed check-ins, an emerging pattern — a named flag appears in your queue with the rule that raised it and the evidence behind it.

Three clear decisions: acknowledge and adapt the plan, acknowledge only, or dismiss. Every response is audit-logged. The system watches between sessions. You decide what happens next.

Inspectable. Conservative. Yours.

Every flag shows its rule

Persistence ≥5d above usual. Sustained deviation >14d. Engagement drop + risk. No black boxes.

Baseline is the patient's own

Rolling 7-day window of self-reports. Compared to themselves, never to a population norm.

You remain the decision-maker

The system never autonomously contacts a patient about a clinical concern. ACE drafts; you sign off.

Audit trail by default

Every acknowledgement, dismissal, and plan change logged with rule, timestamp, and rationale field.

Value drivers

Where the platform

01

High-value talent retention

The most critical lever

Detect sustained strain elevation or early burnout signals early enough for leadership to intervene. Preventing even a handful of high-value exits annually can offset the entire platform investment.

02

Sick-leave reduction

A real, but secondary lever

Even reducing one sick day per employee annually generates meaningful savings at enterprise scale. Sick leave is a visible cost — presenteeism is the larger hidden one.

03

Presenteeism stabilisation

The largest hidden cost

Employees under sustained mental strain remain at work but operate below full cognitive capacity. Small productivity improvements across a large population yield significant returns.
